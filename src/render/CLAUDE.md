# src/render/ — Three.js rendering and generated textures

Shell (Three.js allowed). In `textures/`, `tiles.ts`, `itemIcons.ts`, `cracks.ts` and
`figureTexture.ts` follow the pure-core rules (no `three`, no DOM, no `Math.random`; same
seed, same textures). All textures are generated by code (hard rule 1).

## Files

- `Renderer.ts`, `ChunkMeshes.ts`, `materials.ts` (chunk shader), `Sky.ts`,
  `BreakOverlay.ts` (cracks on the block being broken, one draw call), `RemotePlayers.ts`
  (other players: the animated figure and the name tag, two draw calls each),
  `figureModel.ts` (geometry of six boxes turned in the vertex shader, atlas texture,
  material, slot colours), `FigurePreview.ts` (the turning figure of "Your player", own
  WebGL)
- `textures/`: `tiles.ts` (tile painters), `atlas.ts` (atlas + mip levels),
  `celestial.ts`, `itemIcons.ts` (icons of items that are no blocks), `cracks.ts` (pure),
  `figureTexture.ts` (pure: every look in one atlas, face layout, head icon, `figureView`
  for the sheet, checksum)

The mesher (`src/world/meshing/mesher.ts`) draws a torch with `emitTorch`; its icon is
painted (`itemIcons.ts`); `createItemIcon` prefers a painted icon over the cube.

## Light on screen

Vertex colour: red = face shade × ambient occlusion, green = sky light, blue = block light
(level ÷ 15, averaged over the up to four clear cells that touch the vertex in front of
the face). The vertex shader turns light into a colour factor (`LIGHT_GAMMA`, daylight,
`TORCH_TINT`, the floor `uMinLight` from the Brightness setting); the fragment shader
multiplies. Daylight is `DAYLIGHT_MIN` (0.14) at midnight; the Brightness floor runs 0.02
→ 0.11 (default) → 0.35 in two linear halves (`minLightFor`). Dark values lose more in
pixels than the factor says (sRGB is linear near black): open ground at midnight is about
7 % of noon (AC8.2 in `e2e/night.spec.ts`). `meshChunk` without its third argument is the
mesher from before light (everything open sky), kept for tests. The light itself:
`src/world/CLAUDE.md`. A change here changes world references: `docs/testing.md`,
"Regenerating references of the world".

## The player figure (Phase 6 part D)

Six boxes (legs 4 × 11 × 4, body 8 × 11 × 5, arms 3 × 10 × 3, head 7 × 7 × 7 texels, 29
tall = `PLAYER_HEIGHT`; proportions of this game's own) in one geometry; the vertex shader
turns head and limbs about their pivots from per-figure uniforms, the body's yaw is the
mesh rotation: **two draw calls per figure** (figure and name tag). `figureTexture.ts`
paints all looks into one 128 × 128 atlas (pinned checksum in `figureTexture.test.ts`; it
changes only with an intended change of the looks); the belt is accent (alpha 128 on the
GPU) and tinted with the slot colour, which also stripes the name tag. The looks and the
motion are in `src/player/CLAUDE.md`; the protocol in `src/net/CLAUDE.md`.

Before changing light, the mesher, the chunk shader or the night read the "Decisions and
deviations" of `docs/phase-4a-report.md` and `docs/phase-4b-report.md`; for the figure
`docs/phase-6d-report.md`.
